# Copilot Showed Unexpected Client-Matter Content

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Status:** Draft  
**Source:** `floor6-copilot-access-runbook.md`

If Copilot shows client-matter content you do not expect:

1. Stop and do not keep retrying the prompt.
2. Do not copy, forward, download, or paste the matter content into email, chat, or another tool.
3. Contact the Service Desk through the approved support channel.
4. Tell them your name, computer name, approximate time, matter name or identifier, and the prompt and response. Share details only through the approved incident process.
5. Do not change permissions, remove yourself from groups, delete Copilot history, or try to reproduce the result.

The Service Desk will preserve your report and send it to Security/Data Governance and the relevant Microsoft 365/Copilot and document-management teams. They will provide approved updates. Do not assume the content was correctly or incorrectly available until the investigation confirms the access path.

A floor-wide notice is not issued automatically while the scope is being confirmed.
