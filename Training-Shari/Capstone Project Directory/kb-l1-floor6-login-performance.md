# Floor 6 Sign-in Is Slow or Fails

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Status:** Draft  
**Source:** `floor6-login-performance-runbook.md`

A recent Document Manager update is being investigated because it may make Floor 6 sign-in slow or prevent sign-in.

1. If you are signed in, save your work.
2. Do not uninstall Document Manager or change settings yourself.
3. Do not keep retrying sign-in repeatedly.
4. If the Service Desk asks you to restart, restart once, then try signing in again.
5. If you still cannot sign in, or your computer is still slow, contact the Service Desk.

Include:
- Your name and computer name
- The time the problem happened
- Whether sign-in failed or was just slow
- Any message shown
- Whether nearby Floor 6 users are affected

The IT team may pause the Document Manager rollout and restore an approved version on affected computers. This does not delete your files or change your access. Do not send client or confidential information in an unapproved channel.
