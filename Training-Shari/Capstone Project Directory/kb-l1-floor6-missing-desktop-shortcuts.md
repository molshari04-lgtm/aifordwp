# Missing Desktop Shortcut on Your Floor 6 Computer

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Status:** Draft  
**Source:** `floor6-missing-desktop-shortcuts-runbook.md`

If a work shortcut is missing:

1. Do not create a replacement shortcut yourself.
2. Do not delete or move other desktop items.
3. Contact the Service Desk through the approved support channel.
4. Share your name, computer name, the shortcut name, and when you noticed it was missing.
5. If asked, confirm whether the shortcut is still missing after signing out and signing in again.

The IT team will check whether the shortcut is missing, moved, hidden, or managed by your profile, OneDrive, or company policy. They may restore an approved shortcut and check that it opens the correct application. They will test the change before applying it more widely.

Your files and access are not removed by this process. Do not send confidential client information through an unapproved channel.
