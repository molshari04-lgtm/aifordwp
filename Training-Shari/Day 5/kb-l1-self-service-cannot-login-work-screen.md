Can’t Log In to Your Work Screen? Try This First

If your work screen closes by itself or sends you back to log in, your files and company data are safe. This problem is usually short-term, and your documents are not lost.

Please follow these steps:

1. Close the work program or browser page completely.
2. Wait 2 minutes.
3. Log in again once.
4. If it closes again, restart your laptop.
5. After restart, log in again once.
6. If it still fails, stop trying and contact the IT help team.

What to include in your help request:
- Your work login name
- Time the problem started
- Exact error message (or a screenshot)
- How many times it closed by itself
- Whether it happened right after log in or after a few minutes
- Your location (office/home)
- Your computer name (if known)
- Best contact number

Contact the IT help team if you cannot log in after one restart, if it keeps closing by itself, or if you are unable to continue your work.
