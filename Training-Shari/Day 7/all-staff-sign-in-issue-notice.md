# Important: Sign-in and Account Lockout Issues

We are aware that some colleagues are currently having difficulty signing in or are seeing account lockout messages. Our support team is investigating and working to reduce the impact.

As a temporary workaround, restart your laptop and try signing in once with your full work email and current password. If you recently changed your password, wait five minutes and try again. If it still does not work, open a browser and try signing in to Microsoft 365.

Please avoid repeated sign-in attempts, as these may extend an account lockout. Stay calm and do not worry: the issue is known, and work is underway.

We will provide another update within the next two hours, or sooner if we have important information to share. Please wait for that update before raising a new individual report unless you need urgent assistance with a separate issue.
