## Top 3 To Act On Today (Severity-Weighted Ranking)

Ranking method used: severity first, then affected volume. Blockers are prioritized ahead of Friction and Minor even when blocker count is lower.

### 1) Access lockouts and AVD sign-in failures (Count: 7, Severity: Blocker)

Why this ranks here:
- Highest blocker volume in the dataset.
- Prevents users from working at all and includes repeated lockout patterns across multiple days.

One sentence to manager:
- The top priority is recurring AVD and account lockout failures affecting seven users, because this is a direct cannot-work condition and is creating repeated business disruption.

### 2) OneDrive missing files or sync concern (Count: 4, Severity: Blocker)

Why this ranks here:
- Fewer comments than some friction themes, but potential data-access interruption for active deliverables and urgent meetings.
- Business risk is immediate because users cannot trust file availability.

One sentence to manager:
- OneDrive missing-file incidents are our second priority because four blocker reports indicate immediate delivery risk and possible data-access failure during live business deadlines.

### 3) Shared drive access denied post-migration (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)

Why this ranks here:
- Lower count than printer friction, but this is still cannot-work for core finance workflows (for example month-end reporting).
- Severity outweighs higher-volume friction issues for same-day action.

One sentence to manager:
- Shared-drive access failures rank third because even at three reports they block finance-critical tasks outright, so impact is higher than larger but non-blocking usability complaints.

## Why Not Ranked in Top 3 Today

- Floor 3 printer mapping failures (6, Friction): high volume but users can use floor 2 workaround.
- VPN instability (4, Friction): significant slowdown, but generally degraded rather than total work stoppage.
- UI and navigation confusion (8, Minor): high count but low operational impact.
