## Can't sign in

Restart your laptop and try signing in once with your full work email and current password. If you recently changed your password, wait five minutes and try again.

If it still does not work, open a browser and sign in to Microsoft 365. This checks whether your password works outside the usual sign-in screen.

Contact the service desk if you still cannot sign in or your account is locked. Share your username, the exact message you see, the time of the failed attempt, your device name, and whether Microsoft 365 sign-in worked.

## OneDrive files are missing

Select the OneDrive cloud icon near the clock. If syncing is paused, choose Resume syncing, then check for any problems shown.

Open OneDrive in File Explorer and check the usual folders. You can also visit onedrive.com to see whether the files are stored online.

Contact the service desk if important files are still missing. Share the file names, when you last saw them, whether they appear on onedrive.com, any message shown, and a screenshot of the OneDrive status.

## Shared drive will not open

If you are working away from the office, disconnect and reconnect to your work network, then try the shared drive again. If you are in the office, try opening it without that connection.

In File Explorer, type the shared folder address directly if you know it, then press Enter.

Contact the service desk if access is still denied. Share your username, the drive or folder name, the exact message shown, whether you are in the office or working remotely, and the time you last tried.
