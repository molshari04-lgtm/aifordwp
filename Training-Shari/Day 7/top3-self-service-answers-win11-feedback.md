## 1) Access lockouts and AVD sign-in failures
Restart your laptop, then try signing in once more using your full work email and current password. If you recently changed your password, wait 5 minutes and try again so your account can fully update.

If it still fails, open your browser and sign in to Microsoft 365 to confirm your password works there. If web sign-in works but AVD does not, try AVD one more time after closing and reopening the app.

Contact the service desk if you still cannot sign in after these steps or if your account says locked. Share: your username, exact error message, time of the last failed sign-in, device name, and whether Microsoft 365 web sign-in worked.

## 2) OneDrive missing files or sync concern
Click the OneDrive cloud icon near the clock and select Resume syncing if paused. Then select View sync problems and follow any prompt shown.

In File Explorer, open OneDrive and check if files are in a different folder, such as old desktop or documents folders. You can also sign in at onedrive.com to confirm whether files are online.

Contact the service desk if key files are still missing after these checks. Share: file names, last time you saw them, whether they appear on onedrive.com, any OneDrive error text, and a screenshot of the OneDrive sync status.

## 3) Shared drive access denied post-migration
Disconnect from VPN, wait 30 seconds, then reconnect and try opening the shared drive again. If you are in the office, try once without VPN.

If the drive letter does not open, open File Explorer and type the network path directly if you know it, then press Enter. This can reconnect the drive when the shortcut fails.

Contact the service desk if access is still denied. Share: your username, drive letter or folder path, exact error message, whether VPN is connected, your location (office or remote), and the time you last tried.
