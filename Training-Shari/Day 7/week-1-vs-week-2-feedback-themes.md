# Week 1 vs Week 2 Feedback Themes

## Theme comparison

### Access lockouts and AVD sign-in failures
**Status: Resolved**

Week 2 comments report that login is back to normal and lockouts have stopped: “No more lockouts this week” (7), “Account lockouts have completely stopped” (19), and “Login and lockout issues from week 1 are fully resolved” (34).

### Floor 3 printer mapping failures
**Status: Worsening**

The issue continued throughout week 2. Users said the printer was still broken (5, 18, 35), were still walking to floor 2 (14), and asked for escalation or replacement (9, 28, 40). One comment says it has continued for a third week (23).

### VPN instability during work and calls
**Status: Resolved**

Users reported that the VPN was “rock solid” with no complaints (10), “stable, no drops” (27), and that both VPN and login were solid (36).

### Shared drive access denied post-migration
**Status: Unchanged**

No week 2 comment mentions shared-drive access, either positively or negatively. There is not enough evidence to say that the issue was fixed or became worse.

### OneDrive missing files or sync concern
**Status: Resolved**

Users said their files were showing up normally (3), that there were no more file issues (11, 26, 39), and that syncing was working as expected (21, 32).

### Slow login performance on Win11
**Status: Resolved**

Users said login speed was back to normal (12), fast and reliable (22), and working well without complaints (29).

### UI and navigation changes after migration
**Status: Unchanged**

No week 2 comment refers to Start menu, search, or navigation problems. There is not enough evidence to confirm improvement or resolution.

### Desktop shortcuts or files missing
**Status: Unchanged**

No week 2 comment mentions missing desktop shortcuts or files. The week 2 OneDrive comments confirm files are present, but they do not specifically confirm that desktop items were restored.

### Personalization reset (wallpaper/theme)
**Status: Unchanged**

No week 2 comment mentions wallpaper, theme, or other personal settings. No change can be confirmed.

### Missing Teams visual feature
**Status: Unchanged**

No week 2 comment mentions the missing Teams visual feature. No change can be confirmed.

### Positive migration and performance feedback
**Status: Improving**

Positive feedback continued and became more frequent or stronger in week 2. Users described the experience as “much smoother” (6), reported “great improvement overall” (25), and said everything was smooth or had no issues (17, 38).

## New week 2 theme

### Excel crashes when opening large files

This theme did not appear in the week 1 themes. Users reported crashes or freezes with large spreadsheets, including budget files and files over 10 MB (4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37). The comments describe lost unsaved work and a significant productivity impact, so this should be investigated as a new priority.

## Note on evidence

“Unchanged” means that week 2 provides no clear evidence that the week 1 theme improved, worsened, or was resolved. It does not prove that users are still experiencing the issue.
