# Microsoft 365 Copilot Support: What You Can Do Next

This guide explains what to try when Copilot cannot find, read, or use company information. Copilot follows the access and permissions on your work account, so being able to see something does not always mean Copilot can use it.

## 1. Copilot cannot summarise the Q3 board pack in SharePoint

Copilot may not be able to use the board pack even though you can see the file listed in SharePoint. You may not have permission to open the contents, or the file may not have finished processing after a recent upload or change.

**What to try:**

1. Open the exact board pack directly from SharePoint while signed in with your Finance work account.
2. If you cannot open it, ask the SharePoint folder or file owner to check your access.
3. If you can open it and it was recently uploaded or changed, wait and try Copilot again later.

Contact the service desk if the problem continues. Provide the file name, SharePoint location, the message Copilot displayed, and whether you could open the file directly. Do not send the board pack unless the approved support process requests it.

## 2. Copilot cannot find a new hire's recent emails

Because your account and mailbox are new, your recent email data may still be becoming available to Copilot. Account setup, licensing, or the Outlook account you are using may also need to be checked.

**What to try:**

1. Confirm that the emails are visible in Outlook.
2. Check that Outlook is signed in with your new work account, rather than a personal or previous account.
3. Check whether Copilot can find an older email as well as a recent email.
4. Try again later if the messages were received very recently.

Contact the service desk if older emails are also missing. Provide your start date, mailbox address, approximate email dates, and any message shown. Do not paste confidential email content into an unapproved tool.

## 3. Copilot cannot use a sensitive salary review spreadsheet

The spreadsheet may have a security label or permission that prevents Copilot from using it. This protection is intended to keep sensitive salary information within the people approved to access it.

**What to try:**

1. Open the spreadsheet directly from its approved location.
2. If you cannot open it, ask the HR data owner to confirm whether you should have access.
3. If you can open it but Copilot still cannot use it, contact the service desk or data owner for an access and sensitivity-label check.

Do not remove the security label, change the sharing settings, or copy the spreadsheet to another location to work around the message. When reporting the issue, provide the file name, location, message shown, and the business reason for needing the information. Do not include salary details.

## 4. Copilot cannot find a client contract shared by another organisation

A contract hosted by another organisation may not be available to Copilot in your work environment, even if you received a guest link. Guest sharing does not always give Copilot permission to search or summarise the external content.

**What to try:**

1. Open the guest link directly while signed in with the work account that received the invitation.
2. Confirm that you accepted the guest invitation and that the link has not expired.
3. If the contract is needed in your organisation's Copilot, ask the approved contract owner whether it should be stored in an authorised company location.

Contact the service desk if you can open the contract but Copilot still cannot find it. Provide the sharing organisation, file name or link details through the approved support channel, and the message shown. Do not forward confidential contract content.

## 5. Copilot stopped working for the whole Finance team

Because the issue affects a whole team and started suddenly, a shared account setting, access assignment, or Microsoft 365 service setting may have changed. It may not be a problem with your individual device.

**What to try:**

1. Confirm that you are signed in with your Finance work account.
2. Close and reopen the Microsoft 365 app.
3. Ask one colleague outside Finance whether Copilot is working for them.
4. Note whether the problem affects one app or all Copilot-enabled Microsoft 365 apps.

If the whole Finance team is affected, contact the service desk rather than repeatedly reinstalling apps or changing settings. Provide when the problem started, the affected team, the apps affected, and any message shown.

## 6. Copilot found a file you did not expect

Copilot may have found the file because your account currently has permission to access it, even if you have never opened it. This may be expected inherited access, or it may be an access setting that needs review.

**What to try:**

1. Do not forward, download, edit, or share the file.
2. Check the file and folder permissions, or ask the site owner why you have access.
3. If the file contains sensitive information or the access does not match your role, report it promptly to the service desk or information security team.

Provide the file name, location, and why the access seems unexpected. Do not include the file contents or sensitive information in a report unless the approved reporting process specifically requires it.

## 7. Copilot gives generic answers about company SharePoint content

Copilot may not be able to use the content because of access settings, because the file is still being processed, or because your account or Microsoft 365 app is not fully set up.

**What to try:**

1. Choose one specific SharePoint file that you can open directly.
2. Ask Copilot about that exact file name rather than asking a broad question.
3. Confirm that the file is stored in your organisation's SharePoint or OneDrive.
4. If the file was recently added or changed, try again later.

Contact the service desk if Copilot still cannot use a known, accessible file. Provide the file name, location, when it was added or changed, and the answer Copilot gave. Do not paste internal or confidential content into an unapproved tool.

## 8. Copilot cannot see a shared mailbox calendar

Managing a shared mailbox or acting on behalf of another person does not always give Copilot permission to read that mailbox's calendar. Copilot needs an appropriate level of calendar access.

**What to try:**

1. Open the shared mailbox calendar directly and confirm that you can view it.
2. Ask the mailbox or calendar owner to confirm your calendar permission level.
3. Tell the service desk whether you have view access, delegate access, or only permission to manage the mailbox.

Contact the service desk if you can view the calendar directly but Copilot cannot use it. Provide the shared mailbox address, your access level, the Microsoft 365 app you used, and the message shown. Do not include meeting details unless the approved support process requests them.

## Important

Do not paste payroll, client, legal, security, or other confidential information into an unapproved tool. If Copilot shows you information you should not see, stop using that content and report it immediately through the approved service desk or security reporting route.
