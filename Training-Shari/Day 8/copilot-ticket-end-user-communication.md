# Copilot Support Issues: What to Do Next

We are aware that Copilot may not behave as expected in some situations. In many cases, this is related to access, permissions, account setup, or how recently information was added. Please try the steps below before contacting the service desk.

## 1. Copilot cannot summarise a SharePoint file

Make sure you can open the file yourself from its SharePoint location, then check that you are signed in with your work account. If the file was recently uploaded or changed, wait and try again later.

If Copilot still cannot use the file, contact the service desk. Share the file name, SharePoint location, time you tried, and any message shown.

## 2. Copilot cannot find recent emails

New emails may take some time to become available. Confirm that Outlook is signed in with your work account and that the emails appear in your mailbox. Try again later, especially if you started recently or your account was just created.

Contact the service desk if older emails are also missing. Share your start date, mailbox address, approximate email dates, and any error message.

## 3. Copilot cannot use a sensitive spreadsheet

Open the spreadsheet directly to confirm you have access. Do not change the file's security settings or send a copy to another location to work around the message.

Contact the service desk or your data owner if you need access for your role. Share the file name, location, message shown, and the business reason you need it.

## 4. Copilot cannot find a contract shared by another organisation

Open the guest link directly while signed in with the account that received the invitation. Confirm that the link has not expired and that you accepted the invitation.

If you can open the contract but Copilot cannot find it, contact the service desk. Share the organisation that shared it, the link or file name, and the message shown. Do not forward confidential contract content.

## 5. Copilot stopped working for a team

Check that you are signed in with your work account and close and reopen the Microsoft 365 app. Ask one colleague in another department whether Copilot is working for them.

If the whole team is affected, contact the service desk rather than repeatedly retrying. Share the affected team, when the problem started, which apps are affected, and any message shown.

## 6. Copilot found a file you did not expect

Do not delete or move the file. Check the file and folder permissions, or ask the site owner why you have access. Report any access that does not match your role.

Contact the service desk or information security team promptly if the file contains sensitive information. Share the file name, location, why the access seems unexpected, and a screenshot without including sensitive content.

## 7. Copilot gives generic answers about company information

Ask it about one specific SharePoint file that you can open directly, using the exact file name. Check that the file is in your organisation's SharePoint or OneDrive and was not just added or changed.

Contact the service desk if Copilot still cannot use known, accessible files. Share the file name, location, time it was added or changed, and the answer Copilot gave.

## 8. Copilot cannot see a shared mailbox calendar

Confirm that you can open the shared mailbox calendar directly and that you have permission to view it, not just permission to manage the mailbox. Ask the mailbox owner to confirm your calendar access.

Contact the service desk if you can view the calendar but Copilot cannot use it. Share the shared mailbox address, your level of access, the app you used, and the message shown.

## Important

Do not paste payroll, client, M&A, or other confidential information into an unapproved tool. If you think Copilot has shown you information you should not see, stop using that content and report it immediately through the service desk or security reporting route.
