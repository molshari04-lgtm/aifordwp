# Legal Copilot End-User Communications

## Ticket 1: SharePoint content unavailable

Copilot could not access the requested SharePoint document. Please open the exact file and its parent folder directly in SharePoint, then retry using the file link. If you still cannot open it, contact the Service Desk with the SharePoint location. Do not request or share client documents through an unapproved channel.

## Ticket 2: Outlook case emails not found

We are checking access and indexing for your new account and mailbox. Please confirm that the required case emails are visible directly in Outlook, then retry Copilot after your account and Copilot access have finished provisioning. If the emails are not visible in Outlook, contact the Service Desk with the mailbox and case location. Do not forward confidential case email to personal accounts.

## Ticket 3: Unexpected matter content surfaced

Please stop reviewing or sharing the unexpected content and do not download, copy, or forward it. Report the matter and folder location immediately to the Service Desk and your supervising partner or Legal Information Security contact. Access permissions will be reviewed as a confidentiality precaution. Do not delete or alter the content.

## Ticket 4: Legal team Copilot access outage

We are aware that Copilot access is unavailable for members of the Legal team and are investigating. Please avoid repeated sign-in or license-reset attempts while the access configuration is checked. We will provide the next update through the normal support channel when there is confirmed progress. This is an access incident under investigation, not a confirmed Copilot product fault.

## Ticket 5: Generic answers from contract templates

Please open one known contract template directly, confirm that you can access it, and ask Copilot a question that names the exact file and a distinctive clause. If the response remains generic, send the file location, prompt, and approximate time to the Service Desk. Do not rely on an ungrounded answer for legal or contractual decisions; verify the clause in the source document.

## General guidance

Copilot responses should not replace review of the source document or professional legal judgment. Do not bypass permissions, share confidential material through unapproved channels, or include client or matter details in a support ticket unless the approved secure process requires them.
